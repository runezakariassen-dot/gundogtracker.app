Privacy Policy for Jakthund

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Jakthund is an app for logging hunting dog training, hunting sessions, trials, GPS tracks, notes, dogs, and related activity.

Information we collect

The app may store information that you enter yourself, including dog names, profile images, breed, sex, birth date, notes, hunting logs, training logs, trial logs, GPS tracks, location data during active sessions, photos or media, user account information, sharing information, and subscription status.

How information is used

The information is used to store and display your hunting dog logs, track training and hunting activity, show GPS routes and session history, enable sharing between users, manage app access, and handle subscription status.

Location data

Jakthund may use location data when you start a GPS-based session. Location data is used to record tracks and session history. Location tracking is used only for app functionality related to hunting, training, and trial logs.

Data storage

The app stores data locally on your device and may use cloud services for account, sharing, synchronization, backup, or subscription-related functionality where enabled.

Subscriptions

Jakthund may offer auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple In-App Purchase. Subscription handling and payment processing are managed by Apple.

Third-party services

The app may use third-party services such as Apple App Store, Firebase, or similar infrastructure providers for authentication, storage, synchronization, crash reporting, or app functionality.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only as needed to provide app functionality, such as when you choose to share a dog with another user, or when required by service providers or law.

User control

You may delete or edit data in the app where supported. You may also contact us to request deletion of account-related data.
